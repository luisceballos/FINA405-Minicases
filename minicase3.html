Market conventions

EURUSD means USD units per 1 EUR. A rise means EUR strengthens; a fall means USD strengthens. Multiply EUR amounts by EURUSD to obtain USD. The spot rate applies to conversion today. The forward rate is available today for a conversion in two years; it is not a forecast of the future spot rate. Future market conditions will be revealed only after your first board defense.
